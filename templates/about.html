About CampusConnect

A secure, privacy-first lost and found directory engineered for university campuses.

🛡️

Safe Inquiries

Traditional notice boards expose personal phone numbers and emails publicly.

CampusConnect protects privacy by routing messages through a private inbox that only the verified poster can unlock.

🔐

PIN Authorization

No complex logins or external accounts required. When posting an item, you choose a 4-to-8 digit PIN.

The PIN is securely hashed using PBKDF2/SHA256 so only you can resolve your listing or view messages.

💻

Free Local Stack

Built strictly with standard open technologies:

  • Backend: Python Flask
  • Database: SQLite with parameterized queries
  • Frontend: Responsive CSS and Vanilla JavaScript
  • Cost: $0.00 — Zero paid APIs or subscriptions
⭐

How CampusConnect Works

1

Browse & Search

Search active lost and found listings across campus buildings by keyword, category, location, or date.

2

Report an Item

Submit details for a lost or found possession. You choose a secret 4–8 digit Security PIN to manage the post.

3

Safe Inquiries

Community members send messages through the secure contact form. Direct phone and email remain private.

4

Resolve with PIN

Once an item is safely returned, enter your Security PIN to mark the listing resolved or review inquiry messages.
